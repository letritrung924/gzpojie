FOUR PILLARS

What gives us an unfair advantage.

Decade-deep expertise

Core team averages ten years of industry experience across audio DSP, visual interaction, privacy engineering, and product innovation. We turn complex logic into intuitive products.

Continuous iteration

We never stop polishing. Every release ships with a one-page retro and a three-month post-launch tuning engagement to harden the experience.

Anti-bloat engineering

We refuse to ship dependencies we don't need. A typical gzpojie binary has 4–8x fewer dependencies than market average — fewer supply-chain risks.

Holistic privacy posture

Privacy by architecture, not toggle. The same posture holds whether the app is offline, online, or being reviewed by an EU regulator.

Code-first delivery

Every engagement ships with a working binary inside ten business days — not a hundred-slide deck. Taste the product before paying the second invoice.

Internationally distributed
